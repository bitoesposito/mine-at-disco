The `M` menu is now a music player, and songs can go on discs for a jukebox.

You need Minecraft Java 26.3, Fabric Loader 0.20.1 or newer and Fabric API. Replace the old jar in your `mods` folder with this one.

In this release:

- A player in the menu: play and pause, previous, next, repeat, stop, and a bar you can click or drag to jump through the song.
- A queue of songs to play next, and playlists saved from it.
- A search box for your songs, and lists that scroll with the mouse wheel.
- Volume sliders for Master, Music, Jukebox and Blocks inside the menu.
- YouTube links work in the search, for a video or a whole playlist, and downloads wait in a queue. Download only music you have the right to use.
- Blank discs: craft one from four coal or charcoal around a copper ingot, burn a song onto it from the menu and put it in a jukebox. The song plays from there, fades as you walk away, and the world around it moves to its beat. Burning discs needs a world you play yourself.
- Blocked hits land on the next beat with a bell in the song's key, and a perfect parry plays a chord.

Known limits: one song plays at a time, so a jukebox takes over from the menu's song, and system audio is still experimental.
